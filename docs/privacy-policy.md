# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026

**Operator:** JustGames Digital

**Privacy contact:** support@justgamesdigital.com

This Privacy Policy explains how JustGames Digital ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the JustGames Digital Sessions bot (the "Bot") on Discord.

> **Before publishing:** Publish this document at a stable public HTTPS URL and enter that URL as the Privacy Policy link in the Discord Developer Portal.

## 1. Information We Handle

When you interact with the Bot, we may receive or store:

- **Discord identifiers:** your Discord user ID, the server (guild) ID, channel ID, message ID, and role ID used to provide the Bot's features.
- **Session information:** company name, session date and time, player limit, status, server/location name, description, host name, logo attachment URL, and session ID.
- **Participation information:** whether you joined or left a session and the date and time you joined.
- **Technical information from Discord:** Discord may provide interaction, account, and delivery information needed to process commands, buttons, attachments, and direct messages. The Bot does not intentionally collect your Discord password, payment information, precise location, or message history.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information in a session name, description, server name, host name, or uploaded image. Information you enter in a session may be visible to other members of the Discord server.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- create, display, update, and manage scheduled sessions;
- determine who may host or manage sessions;
- record who joined a session and enforce player limits;
- send session reminders, start instructions, cancellation notices, and end notices through Discord;
- restore pending session buttons after a restart;
- protect the Bot, troubleshoot failures, and respond to requests; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use the information for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information. The Bot has no payment or analytics system.

## 3. Discord and Other Service Providers

The Bot operates through Discord's platform. Discord independently processes information under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We use Discord's API and hosting/infrastructure providers needed to run the Bot and store its database. We do not control how Discord processes information.

The Bot may include a logo URL supplied through Discord. That image may remain hosted and controlled by Discord or the person who supplied it.

## 4. Sharing

We may disclose information:

- to Discord and infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the Bot;
- to server administrators and authorized session hosts through the Bot's commands and messages;
- when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request; or
- when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Bot, or our rights.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

The Bot currently stores session and participation records in a local SQLite database. It does not automatically delete completed, cancelled, or older records. Records may remain until the Bot operator deletes them, removes the database, or changes the retention process.

You may request deletion or correction of information associated with your Discord user ID by contacting `support@justgamesdigital.com`. We may need to verify the request, and some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons. Information in Discord's own systems must be addressed with Discord.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a small Discord bot, including access controls for the Bot credentials and database. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit information that you would not want stored or displayed in the relevant Discord server.

## 7. Children

The Bot is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use Discord in their region. Do not use the Bot if you are not permitted to use Discord.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding your information. Contact us at `support@justgamesdigital.com` to make a request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot or our data practices change. We will update the effective date and publish the revised version at the same policy URL.
